Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables
machines to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.
These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving,
understanding language, and recognizing patterns.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where computers learn from data
instead of being explicitly programmed. Deep Learning is a further
subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks to
solve complex problems such as image recognition and natural language
processing.

AI is widely used in healthcare, finance, education,
transportation, and many other industries.
